How to play

  1. Your knight jumps in an L: two squares one way, then one to the side. It can only land on a lit square, and it puts that light out.
  2. Shadow knights copy each jump you make, in the same direction.
  3. Mirror knights copy it flipped left ↔ right.
  4. Echo knights land anywhere: a lit square goes out, a dark one lights up. If their jump would leave the board, fall into a gap or hit another piece, they stay put.

Turn every square dark to win. Undo is free — and the hint knows when you've hit a dead end.
